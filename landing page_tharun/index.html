Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does BrightLoop manage?

Products and plans, quotations, subscriptions, recurring invoices, payment records, renewal reminders and operational reporting.

Does BrightLoop charge customers directly?

Payments are collected through your existing external payment method. BrightLoop records those payments and tracks invoice balances; it does not charge cards or transfer funds.

How do email verification and reminders work?

BrightLoop supports account verification, password recovery, invitations and invoice emails. Your administrator configures an SMTP provider for delivery and enables scheduling for automated operational jobs.

Where does the workspace run?

The project is self-hosted. Start the BrightLoop application locally, then use Open workspace to visit it at 127.0.0.1:3000. This standalone landing page opens directly in your browser.
